Milky Turmeric Licorice Papaya Bar Soap

Available Now

Brightening • Soothing • Nourishing. Experience the natural goodness of Merlinda's Soaperie's handcrafted Milky Turmeric Licorice Papaya Bar Soap. Carefully made in small batches, this luxurious soap combines the skin-loving benefits of turmeric, licorice root, papaya, goat's milk, and nourishing botanical oils to help promote healthy-looking, radiant skin.

Turmeric and licorice are traditionally used to support a brighter, more even-looking complexion, while papaya provides gentle exfoliation. Goat's milk and shea butter help moisturize and soften the skin, leaving it feeling refreshed and nourished after every use.

Key Benefits

  • Brightens and revitalizes dull-looking skin
  • Gently exfoliates for a smoother appearance
  • Helps soothe and moisturize dry skin
  • Rich, creamy lather
  • Handmade in small batches
  • Made with natural ingredients

Ingredients

Shea butter soap base, infused olive oil (nettle, chamomile, calendula, licorice), goat's milk, turmeric, licorice, papaya, unpasteurized honey, essential oils (frankincense, lemongrass, lavender, sweet orange, bergamot), Vitamin E.

Botanical Hibiscus Herbal Soap

Available Now

Indulge your skin in the lush, floral goodness of Merlinda's Botanical Hibiscus Herbal Soap — a beautifully handcrafted bar bursting with skin-loving botanicals. Hibiscus, rich in natural AHAs, gently exfoliates and firms, while moringa and neem deeply cleanse and purify. Papaya fruit enzymes renew and brighten, and a nourishing blend of shea butter, honey, and infused olive oil leaves skin feeling soft, supple, and radiant.

Each bar is handcrafted with love in Surrey, BC, Canada — made in small batches to ensure quality you can feel from the very first lather.

Key Benefits

  • Firms & brightens with hibiscus natural AHAs and papaya fruit enzymes
  • Cleanses & purifies deeply with moringa and neem
  • Nourishes with shea butter, honey, and infused olive oil
  • Soothes with lemon balm, calendula, and dandelion flowers
  • Scented with lavender and sweet orange essential oils

Ingredients

Soap base (shea butter, honey), hibiscus, bayleaf, papaya fruit & seeds, moringa, neem, lemon & grapefruit zest, lemon balm, calendula, cloves, glycerin, dandelion flowers & roots, cardamom, star anise, infused olive oil, Vitamin E, essential oil (lavender & sweet orange).
